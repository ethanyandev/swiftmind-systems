隐私政策

适用于 swiftmindsystems.com

我们是谁

本网站由 SwiftMind Systems Ltd.（加拿大）运营，用于介绍公司与产品。本政策只说明这个网站本身如何处理信息。

这个网站收集什么

本站是纯静态页面：不设账号、不埋点、不使用 Cookie，也不嵌入第三方统计脚本。页面字体由 Google Fonts 提供，加载字体时你的浏览器会向 Google 发起请求。

本站托管在 Cloudflare。Cloudflare 会为安全防护与请求限流记录访问日志（例如 IP 地址、访问时间、浏览器类型），我们不会主动读取或导出这些日志用于识别个人。

邮件联系

你发邮件到 info@swiftmindsystems.com 时，我们会保存这封往来邮件，只用于回复你，不会用于营销，也不会提供给第三方。

各产品的隐私政策

SaveVia 等产品有各自独立的隐私政策，以产品内与产品官网（例如 savevia.app）上的版本为准。本政策不覆盖产品内的数据处理。

联系方式

任何关于隐私的问题，写信到 info@swiftmindsystems.com。

Privacy Policy

Applies to swiftmindsystems.com

Who we are

This website is operated by SwiftMind Systems Ltd. (Canada) to introduce the company and its products. This policy covers only how the website itself handles information.

What this website collects

The site is static: no accounts, no analytics, no cookies and no third-party tracking scripts. Fonts are served by Google Fonts, so your browser requests them from Google when a page loads.

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Cloudflare keeps access logs (such as IP address, time of request and browser type) for security and rate limiting. We do not read or export those logs to identify individuals.

Email

When you write to info@swiftmindsystems.com we keep the correspondence in order to reply. It is not used for marketing and is not shared with third parties.

Product privacy policies

Products such as SaveVia have their own privacy policies, published inside the product and on the product site (for example savevia.app). Those policies, not this one, govern data handled by the products.

Contact

Questions about privacy go to info@swiftmindsystems.com.
